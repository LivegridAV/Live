Preparation: source albedo converted to WebP; OpenGL normals resized and renormalized; ambient occlusion and roughness packed into R/G channels of lossless WebP. Floor roughness was numerically remapped for a polished finish. No photographic lighting or room views were added. Exact download URLs, hashes and output file sizes are recorded in `provenance.json`; `prepare-materials.mjs` reproduces the preparation.

The optional images in `source-previews/` are ambientCG's CC0 material previews. They are review references and are not loaded by the website.
