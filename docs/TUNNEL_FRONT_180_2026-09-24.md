# Tunnel front-facing 180-degree mapping

The tunnel artwork now spans the forward-facing 180 degrees (yaw −90° to +90° around the tunnel's −Z direction), rather than 360 degrees. Its 2:1 composition uses a matching 90-degree vertical span. Both axes have twice the previous angular source-pixel density, without changing the camera's field of view or generating another upscaled asset.

All LED surfaces still sample one world-space camera ray, so their corners remain continuous. The rear hemisphere is dark; the outermost degree is feathered, and texture coordinates are clamped to prevent edge wrapping. The screen mapping no longer drifts on an animated orbit. The sculptures and dust still animate, and the sculptures retain their independent all-around reflection lighting.

The existing Graphify map confirmed Tunnel imports ImmersiveVolume; direct source inspection isolated the change to its cinematic shader. No other gallery media or geometry changed.

This corrects framing and makes better use of the existing artwork; it does not turn the 1774 × 887 source into native 4K detail.
